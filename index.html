FAQs

Common questions we hear

Answers to what patients most often ask before their first visit to our ENT doctor and sinus specialist team.

What does a sinus specialist actually treat?+

A sinus specialist diagnoses and treats chronic sinusitis, nasal congestion, sinus pressure, and structural issues like a deviated septum — going beyond what general allergy medication can resolve.

When should I see a doctor about hearing loss?+

If you're asking people to repeat themselves, raising the TV volume, or struggling in noisy rooms, it's time for a hearing loss evaluation so we can identify the cause and the right therapy.

Do you evaluate tonsils and adenoids in children?+

Yes. We perform adenoid evaluation and tonsil adenoid assessments for children, including cases linked to breathing difficulties, snoring, or speech delay.

Can snoring be a sign of sleep apnea?+

Often, yes. Persistent snoring can point to sleep apnea. Our snoring therapy evaluation looks at your airway to determine the cause and the most effective treatment.

How do I schedule an appointment?+

Simply call us at 646-777-8256. Our Brooklyn office at 202 Quentin Rd, Suite 2B is ready to schedule your ENT, sinus, or hearing consultation.
